# New Group 2 — Full Community & Social Layer

## Objective

Turn `المجتمع` from a thin discussion area into a real Makkah community system that supports durable local knowledge, repeated groups, conversations and pathways into real-world participation.

## Cumulative rule

This group sits on top of:

**Old cumulative baseline + New Group 1 inventory/discovery foundation.**

Do not rebuild Community as a disconnected mini-app.

## Eight top-level community families

Keep these as the stable high-level structure:

1. الأحياء
2. زوار مكة
3. الحج والعمرة
4. المطاعم والتجارب
5. الثقافة والتاريخ
6. التطوع والمبادرات
7. التعليم والهوايات
8. الحياة في مكة

Under them, create many actual communities, clubs and topic groups rather than eight empty containers.

## Core distinctions

### Community
A durable people/knowledge space.

### Club
A repeated participation group, e.g. a recurring interest or activity group.

### Activity
A specific thing someone can go do.

These should connect, not collapse into one object.

## Main chain

**Community → Conversation → Activity → Join → Attend → Continue**

The product should let local knowledge naturally lead into participation, and participation naturally generate more useful community knowledge.

## Contribution types

Use the unified Contribution model for:

- questions
- answers
- recommendations
- experience reports
- photos
- updates
- discussions
- tips
- corrections

## Required community behaviors

- join / leave / follow communities
- view community detail
- view threads
- ask questions
- answer
- recommend
- post experience reports
- add photos
- mark useful/helpful
- link places/restaurants/events/activities
- move from a conversation to a Decision Page
- move from a community/club to a real activity
- continue discussion after attendance

## Participation context

Activities/clubs should clearly communicate context such as:

- مناسب للحضور منفردًا
- مناسب للمبتدئين
- مجموعة صغيرة
- للعائلات
- للنساء — only where legitimately applicable
- لغة النشاط
- عدد المشاركين — only when it is actual/known prototype data, not fabricated as live truth
- أول مرة؟ هذا ما تتوقعه

## Community home

Community home should feel like a real feed/community product, not eight equal cards.

Use editorial mix:

- active local questions
- useful recent answers
- activity prompts
- community photos
- neighborhood updates
- recurring club activity
- new contributor prompts
- relevant discussions attached to places or plans

## Community detail

A community detail page should communicate:

- what this group is about
- who/what it is for
- current useful conversations
- active/relevant activities
- local recommendations
- lightweight rules
- contribution CTA

## Thread behavior

Threads should preserve human voice and context.

Do not force every contribution into formal MSA. Translation can be offered separately.

Each thread should retain:

- author / contribution context
- source type
- time
- linked object if any
- useful/helpful actions
- replies
- moderation/report affordances

## Cross-linking

Community content should connect to:

- Places
- Restaurants
- Experiences
- Events
- Activities
- Offers
- Providers
- Plan

Example:

A question about a Hira visit can open Hira's Decision Page. A club discussion can lead into an Activity. A completed activity can feed back into the community.

## Privacy and anti-creep boundary

Do **not** build:

- nearby-user radar
- public exact user locations
- random stranger matching
- “people physically near you” discovery
- Tinder-for-friends mechanics
- public attendee identity by default

The social layer should feel useful and community-led, not invasive.

## Acceptance criteria

At the end of Group 2:

- all eight families contain meaningful sub-communities/topics
- a user can ask, answer, recommend and contribute
- conversations link to inventory objects
- communities link to activities
- community pages are visibly different from generic content cards
- users can follow/join without being forced into public identity exposure
- community Arabic retains natural voice
- no creepy proximity mechanics are present
- New Group 1 browsing and old journeys still work
