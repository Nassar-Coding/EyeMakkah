Thank you, Dr. Amr, for today’s productive one-to-one meeting.

Based on Dr. Amr’s discussion with His Excellency Minister of Haj and Umrah the main direction is to refocus the platform around Makkah residents and visitors, rather than addressing multiple audiences at this stage.

The updated concept should focus on building a Makkah digital community, where residents and visitors can:

- Access and discover relevant services through one integrated experience.
- Interact with services and with the wider community.
- Build personalized journeys based on their needs, activities, and interactions.
- Generate meaningful engagement and interaction data that can support future insights.

From a platform perspective, the revised proposal should address both the application and the backend capabilities. The application will enable the community experience and customer journeys, while the backend will capture and analyze community interactions to support Business Intelligence, analytics, and future decision-making.

Next Step

Based on this new direction and Dr. Amr’s recommendations, the team will rework the current proposal and prototype, with a stronger focus on the Makkah resident and visitor community, the services and journeys offered to them, and the intelligence that can be generated from their interactions.

An updated version will be prepared internally and shared with the group for review.
