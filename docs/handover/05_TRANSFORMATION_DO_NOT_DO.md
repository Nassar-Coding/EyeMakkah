# EyeMakkah — Do Not Do

Do not:

- publish to GitHub unless explicitly asked
- deploy to Vercel unless explicitly asked
- start Deep Research unless explicitly asked
- replace cumulative work with a smaller visual showcase
- turn EyeMakkah into a government portal or institutional dashboard
- create a separate AI tab just because AI exists
- make every section a rounded card
- use generic “Verified” badges without provenance
- promise booking confirmation from an outbound link
- promise APIs or live callbacks that are not confirmed
- expose exact attendee/user locations publicly
- build creepy nearby-user discovery or stranger matching
- infer health, religion, identity, nationality or sensitive traits from behavior
- turn subjective community opinions into objective facts
- make one dismissal a permanent preference
- allow expired content to remain promoted
- present a generic stock image as a specific real restaurant/event
- homogenize all community Arabic into one AI voice
- use repeated generic Kaaba imagery as the main content strategy
